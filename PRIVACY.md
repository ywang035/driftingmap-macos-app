# Privacy Policy

**Drifting Map** is a macOS app that displays a slowly drifting map as your desktop wallpaper, developed by Yang Wang.

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

This policy describes the released version 1.0 of Drifting Map.

## Summary

Drifting Map has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It doesn't run its own servers, and it doesn't collect or store your personal information anywhere except on your own Mac.

## Information the App Does Not Collect

Drifting Map does not collect, transmit, or store:

- Your name, email address, or any account/profile information — there is no sign-up or login.
- Analytics or usage data.
- Advertising identifiers, and it shows no ads.
- Your device's precise location. The app does not request or use Location Services.
- Payment or billing information. Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or trials.

## Information Handled Through Apple's Services

The released app's network features are handled by Apple's own frameworks, not by us:

- **City search.** When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Apple's MapKit service to find matching places and resolve a location — the same technology behind Maps and other Apple apps. This is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Apple Maps.** The app requests map snapshots directly through Apple's native MapKit framework for the region currently displayed. These requests are handled by Apple and are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not proxy the requests through a server or receive the request data. The app estimates colour-region masks from snapshots and temporarily caches those masks and recoloured images in memory; it does not save or export them to disk.

## Information Stored Only On Your Device

The app saves your preferences — chosen city, palette, light or dark colour mode, zoom, drift speed, direction, and mouse-follow setting — inside the app's own sandboxed storage on your Mac. It also remembers whether you have dismissed the welcome screen. These preferences are not uploaded to us. Your chosen map region is used in requests to Apple Maps, as described above.

When mouse following is enabled, the app reads the pointer position locally to choose a drift direction. Pointer positions are not recorded or transmitted. The app also reads system uptime to measure animation transitions. These timing values are used locally and are not transmitted.

Temporary map image caches are held in memory and discarded when the app quits. macOS may retain local preferences after the app is deleted; you can remove the app's local container to delete those preferences.

## Children's Privacy

Drifting Map is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Data Retention & Deletion

Because we don't operate any servers that store your information, there's nothing on our end to request or delete. The app's saved preferences live on your own device. Apple's services handle their own data retention under Apple's policies.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the revised policy. A copy of the policy is included in the app and can be read from About → Privacy Policy without a network connection.
